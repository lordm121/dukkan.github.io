About Smart POS

Built by business owners, for business owners. Here is everything you need to know about how our system works.

Technical Architecture

Smart POS is built on a modern, high-performance offline-first architecture. Instead of relying on slow web servers, all your inventory, sales, and financial data is stored locally on your device's encrypted database. This guarantees zero latency during checkout and 100% uptime, even during internet outages.

Data Sync & Backup

When you are connected to the internet, our background sync engine securely mirrors your encrypted data to your personal cloud account. We use end-to-end encryption (E2EE), meaning we cannot read your business data. Only your authorized devices possess the decryption keys.

Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026. Your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Smart POS operates fundamentally as a local-first application. The vast majority of your data (inventory, sales records, customer details, budgets) is stored directly on your physical device. We do not collect, transmit, or store this data on our servers unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync.

2. Cloud Synchronization

If you utilize our optional Cloud Sync feature to backup data or share it across multiple devices, your data is encrypted locally before transmission. We utilize AES-256 end-to-end encryption. The keys to decrypt this data remain on your devices; Smart POS personnel cannot access your business metrics.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties. We may use anonymous, aggregated crash reports (via services like Firebase Crashlytics) strictly to improve application stability. You can opt out of crash reporting in the app settings.

4. Your Data Rights

You retain full ownership of your data. You have the right to export your entire database at any time (via CSV or JSON). You also have the right to request immediate deletion of any synced data from our servers via the "Delete Account & Data" option within the application.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@smartpos.app.
